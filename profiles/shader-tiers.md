# Shader tiers

Use shaders only with Oculus installed. Put the downloaded shader archive, still zipped, in `shaderpacks`.

| Tier | Shader choices | Starting preset | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Very low | MakeUp Ultra Fast; Sildur's Enhanced Default Lite | Potato or Low | Integrated graphics and older laptops |
| Low | Complementary Reimagined; Sildur's Vibrant Lite | Potato or Low | Entry-level dedicated GPUs |
| Medium | Complementary Reimagined; BSL | Medium | Mainstream gaming GPUs |
| High | Complementary Reimagined; BSL; Photon | High, then tune | Modern high-end GPUs |

For Lite, use Very low first and keep shaders disabled if frame time is unstable. For Power, use Medium as the default and High only after testing a new world, a village, a dungeon, and TACZ combat. Reduce shadow resolution, volumetric lighting, reflections, ambient occlusion, and render distance before reducing the entire shader preset.

Shader names and supported Minecraft versions change. Download the exact 1.20.1-compatible release from each author's official project page and follow its license.
