# Lite profile settings

- Allocate 5 to 6 GB RAM; close other memory-heavy applications.
- Render distance: 6 to 8 chunks.
- Simulation distance: 4 to 6 chunks.
- Graphics: Fast.
- Entity distance: 75% or lower.
- Particles: Decreased or Minimal.
- Clouds: Off.
- Entity shadows: Off.
- Mipmap levels: 2 to 4.
- Disable shader packs by default. For supported hardware, test MakeUp Ultra Fast or Sildur's Enhanced Default Lite at the lowest preset.
- Sound Physics Remastered is omitted by default to reduce audio processing overhead. This improves performance only if audio processing was a bottleneck; measure on the target machine.
- Keep only one major terrain overhaul and a restrained set of structure mods.
- Prefer a fresh world after changing terrain or biome generation mods.
