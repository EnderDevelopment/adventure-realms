# Power profile settings

- Allocate 10 to 12 GB RAM and keep Java 17 selected.
- Render distance: 12 to 16 chunks.
- Simulation distance: 8 to 10 chunks.
- Graphics: Fabulous only when the shader loader and GPU are stable; otherwise Fancy.
- Entity distance: 100%.
- Particles: All.
- Clouds: Fast or shader controlled.
- Use Embeddium and Oculus settings before increasing shader quality.
- Begin with a medium shader preset. Move to high only when frame times remain stable in a dense village and during combat.
- Enable Sound Physics Remastered at default quality. Disable it if audio processing causes stutter.
- Keep TACZ weapon and attachment packs aligned with the installed TACZ version.
- Test world generation in a new world after any terrain mod change.
